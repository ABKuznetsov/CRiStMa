Scientific reference: B. Cordero, V. Gómez, A. E. Platero-Prats, M. Revés,
J. Echeverría, E. Cremades, F. Barragán and S. Alvarez, “Covalent radii
revisited”, Dalton Transactions (2008), 2832–2838.
DOI: `10.1039/B801115J`.

The complete pinned artifact is preserved: 101 records for 96 elements from H
through Cm, including the published carbon hybridization and Mn/Fe/Co spin
variants. No value is invented for elements absent from the source. For a
generic element lookup CrIStMa returns the largest published variant, which is
a conservative search envelope when bonding or spin is not known yet. Exact
variants remain available through `find_variants()`.

Covalent radii provide the oxidation- and coordination-independent first-pass
scale for geometric candidate enumeration. Shannon radii are applied later as
secondary ionic-distance evidence once oxidation state and candidate
coordination are available; they do not replace the initial search scale.
